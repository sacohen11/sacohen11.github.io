Hi, I’m Sam.

I built this site as a home for the things I spend the most time thinking about: running, tennis, and the weirdly fast-moving world of AI.

I run marathons and ultras, still genuinely love playing tennis, and spend a lot of time trying new AI tools to see which ones are actually useful and which ones are just marketing with extra steps.

I’m also learning a bit of Spanish, so if you catch a random *hola* or *vamos* around here, that’s why. Hablo un poquito de español.

This site is still a work in progress, but the goal is simple: share race reports, training notes, and whatever else seems worth keeping.
